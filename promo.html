Good to know

Is the speed guaranteed?

Is there a data limit?

No quota. We may manage traffic that harms other users.

Do I get the deposit back?

Yes. RM250 is refunded when you return the modem in good condition (less any damage).

What if I stop early?

Short packages are non-refundable once activated. Longer packages can be refunded for unused months under our terms.
